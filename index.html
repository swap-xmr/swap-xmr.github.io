Swap XMR: choose your pair, send, receive

  1. Choose your payout

    Open the dashboard, enter your XMR amount and select BTC, USDT or LTC. Paste the receiving address for that asset and network.

  2. Check, then send

    Review the receive amount, fees, minimum and quote expiry. Send the requested XMR amount to the deposit address shown for your order.

  3. Receive your crypto

    The service releases the asset after your Monero transaction confirms and the order completes processing. Follow the payout transaction to your wallet.

XMR swap costs: network fee, service fee and payout

Sending fee
Your Monero wallet charges a network fee to send XMR. Keep enough available to cover it.
Swap cost
A service fee or exchange-rate spread affects the quote. Compare the final receive amount for the same XMR input.
Payout fee
The destination network adds its own transaction cost. Check whether it is included in the receive amount.

How long does a swap XMR order take?

Monero targets a block about every two minutes. Completion also depends on the required confirmations, service processing and the payout network.

A two-minute block is not a two-minute swap. Use your order status to follow each stage.

Why is my XMR swap pending?

Deposit still pending

Check the transaction ID and confirmations. If it is confirmed but the order has not updated, contact the order’s support with the order ID and transaction hash.

Balance is locked

Let your Monero wallet finish syncing and check its unlocked balance. Recently received XMR and change can need more blocks before they are spendable.

Quote expired or amount changed

Refresh an expired quote before sending. If you already sent an incorrect amount, keep the transaction ID and resolve the existing order with support.

Swap Monero: what stays private?

Monero is a privacy-focused layer-one blockchain. RingCT hides transaction amounts; stealth addresses protect recipients on-chain. Read the official explanation of Monero’s privacy technology.

When you swap XMR to BTC, USDT or LTC, the payout follows the destination network’s visibility rules. Use a fresh receiving address where supported and keep your order details private.

XMR swap essentials: minimums, rates and receiving funds

How do I swap XMR to BTC?

Select XMR as the sending asset and BTC as the receiving asset. Enter a Bitcoin mainnet address, review the quote and send the requested Monero deposit. The service releases BTC after confirmations and order processing.

Can I swap XMR without an account?

Account requirements depend on the service handling the order. Open the dashboard and review its requirements before sending XMR. An account-free flow does not guarantee that verification will never be requested.

Which network should I use to swap XMR to USDT?

Send XMR on the Monero network. For USDT, choose a payout network offered in the quote that your receiving wallet supports. The network must match at both ends of the USDT payout.

What is the minimum amount to swap XMR?

The minimum depends on the pair, network costs and current quote. Check the order’s displayed minimum before sending and leave enough XMR in your wallet for the sending fee.

Why is my swap XMR order pending?

A pending order can be waiting for the Monero deposit, enough confirmations, service processing or payout confirmation. Check your transaction hash and order status. For a confirmed deposit that is not credited, share the order ID and transaction hash with support.
